# Privacy Policy for Idle Clicker

**Effective Date:** Oct 07, 2026

Thank you for playing **Idle Clicker**, developed by **BC Studio**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following information:

### Gameplay Information

* Anonymous player identifier (UUID)
* Game progress and levels
* Scores, achievements, and statistics
* Coins, rewards, tap counts
* Gameplay history

### Device Information

* Device model
* Operating system version
* App version
* Language and region settings
* IP address (processed by our service providers)
* Crash reports and diagnostic information

### Account Information

We auto generate the player profile by using UUID. No sign-in required to play the game. We process the following informaiton

* Display name
* Email address
* Profile picture (if permitted by you)

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

* Save and synchronize game progress
* Improve gameplay and game balance
* Analyze game performance and user engagement
* Detect bugs and crashes
* Prevent cheating and fraudulent activity
* Display advertisements
* Improve future versions of the game

## Advertising

Idle clicker or idle empire may display advertisements through **Google AdMob**.

AdMob may collect information such as:

* Advertising ID
* Device information
* Approximate location
* Interaction with advertisements

This information helps deliver relevant ads, measure ad performance, and reduce fraudulent activity.

You can learn more by visiting Google's Privacy Policy.

## Analytics

We may use **Google Firebase Analytics** to understand how players use Idle Clicker/Empires.

Firebase Analytics may collect information including:

* Screens viewed
* Session duration
* Device information
* Game events
* Crash information
* Performance metrics

This information helps us improve the game experience.

## Cloud Services

Gameplay data may be securely stored using cloud services such as:

* Supabase
* Cloudflare
* Google Firebase (if enabled)

These providers process data on our behalf to operate the game.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information only with trusted service providers that help us operate the game, including:

* Google
* Supabase
* Cloudflare

These providers are required to process data according to their own privacy and security standards.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

## Data Retention

We retain gameplay information only for as long as necessary to operate and improve Idle Clicker/Empire or as required by law.

## Children's Privacy

Idle Clicker/Empire is intended for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.
